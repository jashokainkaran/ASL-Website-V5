# Particle Reference Notes

Source: an 8.7 second phone recording of a laptop screen showing a third-party interactive particle site. Frames `ref_01` to `ref_08` were taken at 0.5s, 1.5s, 2.5s, 3.5s, 4.5s, 5.5s, 6.5s and 7.5s, cropped to the screen.

These are BEHAVIOURAL references only. Do not reproduce the artwork, shapes, text, layout, branding or colour grade. The source brand name is deliberately not recorded here.

Footage caveats: it was filmed off a screen at an angle, so there is glare, a blue colour cast, softness and motion blur. Judge motion, structure and particle behaviour, not exact brightness or colour. ASL uses a space-black background with warm bone particles instead.

## Frame by frame

- **ref_01 (0.5s):** a coherent formation (an X) built from a dense mass of very fine particles. A loose fringe of scattered particles streams off its edges. A few large, soft, out-of-focus discs sit near the camera. Text sits at the far edges, leaving the formation in open space.
- **ref_02 (1.5s):** mid-transition. The formation is smearing into a ribbon-like mass, not cutting or fading. Colour shifts across the mass. A faint wireframe volume surrounds it.
- **ref_03 (2.5s):** the pointer drags a thin, bright, tapered ribbon of light through space in long looping curves. The head is brightest and the tail fades. It reads as a continuous line made of dense points, not separate dots.
- **ref_04 (3.5s):** a new coherent formation (a hand) inside a faint grid volume. Edges are brighter and the interior has grain. Headline text sits in the surrounding negative space.
- **ref_05 (4.5s):** large ribbons loop across depth, some very close to the camera. Each ribbon has a halo of dispersed fine particles around it and streaks that suggest velocity.
- **ref_06 (5.5s):** the matter becomes a wide terrain or wave of points seen in perspective. Particles are visibly larger near the camera and smaller in the distance, with a range of sizes and brightness. Content panels are composed beside it.
- **ref_07 (6.5s):** the matter is dissolved to a thin sparse stream in a mostly empty frame, ready to gather again. This is a deliberate moment of stillness.
- **ref_08 (7.5s):** a new formation (a tree) clumps into cloud-like masses with fine tendrils, again within a faint grid volume with a label above and form fields composed around it.

## Mechanics worth translating

1. One particle population keeps changing form. Nothing cuts or fades.
2. Formations are dense and grainy, with brighter edges and a loose fringe of strays.
3. Transitions smear and flow through ribbon-like intermediate shapes.
4. Strong size and depth variation: tiny fine points, a few large soft discs near the camera, depth-dependent size and brightness.
5. Streaks along the direction of motion during fast movement.
6. The pointer leaves a long, tapered, glowing trail, not just a local push.
7. Large areas of empty space and moments of near-stillness between formations.
8. HTML type sits in the negative space beside the scene, spatially composed with it.
9. A faint grid or hairline volume around a formation (optional; keep very restrained for ASL).

## Do not take

The blue and violet colour grade, the specific shapes (X, hand, tree), the words, the panels and cards, the brand, and the layout.
